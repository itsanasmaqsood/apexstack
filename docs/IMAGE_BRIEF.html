ApexStack Image Brief

Every image slot on the site, led by the exact words that appear on or beside it. The picture has to illustrate that sentence — that is the whole point. An image that could sit under any heading is the wrong image.

Rules for every generated image:

  • No people. No faces, hands, figures or silhouettes.
  • No text, letters, numbers or logos inside the picture. Anything an AI writes comes out misspelt, and the site lays its own real text on top.
  • Brand palette only: near-black #08090A, lime #B4CC04, hot lime #D4F005, plus greys and white. No blue, purple, orange or teal — that mix is what currently makes the homepage look like a different company's site.
  • Dark. Every one of these sits on a near-black page.
  • Send PNG or JPG at the exact pixel size given. I handle conversion and compression.

Sizes are already doubled for retina.
